1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you interact with our Pinterest automation tool ("Hermes Pin Bot"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data We Access

Our application accesses the following Pinterest data through the official Pinterest API:

  • Your Pinterest boards (read & write)
  • Your pins (read & write)

This access is used solely to create and schedule pins on your behalf.

3. Data Storage

We do not store your Pinterest credentials or personal information on external servers. All operations run locally on your machine. Pin content and metadata are stored temporarily in local CSV files during processing.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Pinterest API — for posting pins
  • Cloudinary — for hosting product images (public CDN)
  • Amazon Product Advertising API — for product data (affiliate links)

Each service has its own privacy policy. We do not share your personal data with these services beyond what is necessary for functionality.

5. Your Rights

You can revoke API access at any time via Pinterest Settings → Apps. Deleting the application from your Pinterest account will immediately stop all automated posting.

6. Data Retention

Local CSV files and images are retained on your device until you manually delete them. No data is sent to external servers except API requests to Pinterest and Cloudinary.

7. Security

API tokens are stored in local .env files and never transmitted to third parties. We recommend keeping your .env file secure and never sharing it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective Date" at the top indicates the last revision.

9. Contact

For questions or concerns, contact us at kangmaswawan21@gmail.com.
