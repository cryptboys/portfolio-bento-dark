Privacy Policy — play and learn

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. What this app is

play and learn is a personal application that connects to the owner's own Pinterest business account through the official Pinterest API. Its sole purpose is to create and schedule promotional Pins for the owner's children's activity book products.

2. Data we access

Through Pinterest OAuth, the app may access:

  • Account profile information (username, profile data)
  • Boards — read and write (to select where Pins are published)
  • Pins — read and write (to create promotional Pins)

Access is limited to the authenticated user's own Pinterest account. The app does not access any other user's Pins, boards, or data.

3. How we use the data

Data is used exclusively to create, schedule, and manage promotional Pins for the owner's own products. Data is never used for any other purpose.

4. Data storage and security

OAuth access tokens are stored securely in an encrypted credential vault and are never written to logs, files, or chat transcripts. Tokens are used only for authenticated requests to Pinterest's official API.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

6. Children's privacy

This app is a backend publishing tool operated by an adult and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.
